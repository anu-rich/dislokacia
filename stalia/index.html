What it counts

Each rule below is a setting you choose for every port.

Working time
SHINC, SHEX, SSHEX, FHEX, Saturday noon to Monday 08:00, or any weekly period you define. Holidays with their own eve and next-morning times. Unless used or even if used.
Commencement
A fixed turn time after NOR, or the Gencon rule: notice by noon starts laytime at 13:00, later notice starts it on the next working day. Or always the next working day at a set time, such as 08:00. NOR in office hours only. Unless sooner commenced.
Interruptions
Rain, shifting, awaiting pilot, breakdowns, surveys, strikes. Count each at 0%, 50% or any share, and say whether it still applies once the vessel is on demurrage.
Between ports
Reversible, non-reversible or averaged laytime. A vessel that arrives already on demurrage is charged from NOR, or after notice time if the charter says so.
Money
Demurrage pro rata. Despatch at half rate or your own, on working time saved or all time saved. Commission deducted.
Paper
A day-by-day time sheet and a statement with the terms applied, the facts, the totals and two signature lines. In English, Chinese, Russian, Kazakh, Ukrainian, Turkish or Greek.

Questions

Is the calculator really free?
Yes. Calculations, the time line and the time sheet are free and unlimited. Pro adds your letterhead, a clean PDF, exports and an unlimited library.
Does it work for tankers?
Yes. Enter the hours allowed, for example 72 hours in total, choose reversible laytime, set six hours after NOR “unless sooner commenced”, and add shifting or pumping delays as interruptions.
Where are my calculations stored?
In your browser. Nothing is uploaded. “Backup file” moves a calculation to another computer, and “Copy link” sends it to a colleague.
Can I rely on the result in a claim?
Use it as you would use your own spreadsheet. The arithmetic is tested, but the charter party governs: check which exceptions apply and whether they apply on demurrage.
How do I get Pro?
Buy online by card and the licence key arrives by e-mail; paste it into the calculator. For an invoice or a bank transfer, write to us.
Which languages are there?
The calculator and the statement come in English, Chinese, Russian, Kazakh, Ukrainian, Turkish and Greek. The statement can be in a different language from the screen.
Is it safe to upload a charter party?
You decide what leaves your device. Black out names, rates and anything confidential first: the marks are painted into the page image, and only those images are sent to be read by an AI model. We do not keep them.
How reliable is the reading?
It flags whatever it could not find or was unsure about. You confirm the result and can correct any value by hand, exactly as if you had typed it yourself. Handwriting and poor photos need a closer look.
